# 0006 Change the goal from "reproducing the author" to "our research loop", and stop

**Situation.** The round 1 scoring gave all three posts about 50% of development units and 35 to 58% of rare sources. This was with a profile that still carried traces of the original. Meanwhile the reproduced posts were longer than the originals and opened primary materials directly, so their sources were of higher quality. Reaching the author criteria of 90/80 would mean fixing and rerunning method extraction several times, and that process teaches the worker to cut things out, not to find things.

**Decision.** Change the goal to "a loop that takes one topic, finds the backward and forward links with their sources, and writes a post in which every fact reaches a source". The pass thresholds are our criteria (valid, fabrication 0, a chain in all three directions, at least 20 sources with at least 5 primary). The reference-author criteria are only reported. Score up to the round 2 (hold-out) attempt, and do not run round 3.

**Rejected options.** Iterate until the author criteria pass: the cost is high, and what we gain is imitation of style and omission.

**Result.** Method re-extraction and the step of adding kind and search method to the site ledger moved to the next cycle.
