# 0005 Block the use of pages after the date, not the viewing

**Situation.** A reproduction worker must not use material from after the original's publication time. The first rule was "if you opened it, the attempt is invalid". But search results show no dates, so you have to open a page to know, and the more diligent the worker, the more contacts it makes.

**Decision.** A contact that was closed after a date check is allowed and only recorded. For a contact where the body was read, the verification worker checks whether a fact found only on that page entered the result, and if so the attempt is invalid. If the source list contains a page from after the date, the attempt is invalid. If the worker read the body of an author domain or a page quoting the original, the attempt is invalid as before.

**Rejected options.** Invalid as soon as it is opened: "the possibility of having been influenced by reading" cannot be measured, and what cannot be measured cannot be a rule. The existing check, "every fact must reach a source, and every source has a date", already catches the same risk.

**Result.** Two of the three round 1 reproductions were invalid under the old rule and became valid under the new one.
