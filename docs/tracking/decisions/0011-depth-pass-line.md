# 0011 A reproduction may be broader than the original, never shorter: the depth pass line

**Situation.** The first two test posts (containers, rare earths) were reproduced validly and with sources, but both chains were shallower than the originals: the reproduction stopped at the industry level where the author went on into war logistics, politics, a method, a law. The user's rule: extra links are welcome, a shorter chain is a fail. The previous profile's stop rule ("stop when one more step no longer changes the understanding") let the worker stop early.

**Decision.** Depth is measured per direction as the number of chain steps before and after the main topic, counted by the comparison worker from its own unit lists for both posts. `rek compare-summary` computes a verdict from `comparison.md`: valid, fabricated 0, backward depth ≥ original, forward depth ≥ original; units covered, facts and extra links are reported only. The B and F stop rules of the profile are taken from where the author actually stopped in every material post (the farthest unit of each chain), stated as general moves without naming kinds of endpoints; the container post was fully traced and became the fourth material post. Image descriptions, qualifiers, connective phrases (reasons, time relations, labels) and attributions (who, which office, which year) are facts that need a source. A failing post is fixed at the responsible stage and rerun, at most three attempts.

**Rejected options.** Measuring depth as reaching the original's farthest endpoint: the user chose step counts. Naming endpoint kinds in rules (a war, a law, a person): the user refused; depth had to come from the stop wording and more evidence. Rerunning the rare-earth post for a before/after: a series post whose depth leans on earlier posts. Adding a depth criterion to `/rek:research`: no original to compare with.

**Result.** With the four-post profile (58 rules, hash fe7b2c5cf535) depth passed on every one of six attempts across the two test posts, by a wide margin (backward 3→12 and 9→15, forward 8→17 and 8→12 on the last attempts). Neither post passed the pass line: fabrication went 4 → 4 → 1 on the housing post and 4 → 1 → 2 on the nuclear post, every item an unsourced specific inside a sourced sentence (a motive, a ministry name where the source said LH, a minister's office, a year, a given name joined to a surname, "spa town"), never a number or an event. The attempt budget ran out with both posts at fail; the routing says reproduction every time and method extraction nowhere on the pass-line conditions. The remaining work is on the researcher's last-mile discipline, not on the profile.
