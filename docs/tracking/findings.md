# Unsolved problems

- **The rare-source verdict depends on the region of the search tool.** The workers' web search tool is US-based, so even Korean searches return almost no Korean outlets. As a result almost every domestic article is classified as rare (rare counts under the reference-author criteria: 32, 33, 39). Dozens of rare sources for one blog post means the definition is loose. Why it cannot be solved now: no tool available to workers returns Naver search results in the same format. Approach: fetch the top 10 domains of Naver News and integrated search by code, merge them into the set, and recount the rare sources.
- **Chrome extension tools were not visible in subagents.** Paid and 403 pages (로이드 리스트 (Lloyd's List), FT, Seatrade, and others) were reached only through summaries, reposts, and the Wayback Machine. Why it cannot be solved now: it appears to be a tool exposure problem in this session environment, and the conditions to reproduce it were not confirmed. Approach: rerun one source tracing in a session where the Chrome extension is visible, and check whether the `[inferred]` sources decrease.
- **The profile carries traces of the material posts and contaminates the tests.** The hold-out variants removed them, but traces remain in shared documents, as in the development unit example in `evidence.md` ("청어 붕괴 → 북해 고등어 → …"). Why it cannot be solved now: there are only three material posts, so a complete separation is impossible. Approach: test on a new post (one not used as material) and replace the examples in the shared documents with other topics.
- **Evidence marks remain in the body of the round 2 reproduced posts.** The brief was fixed after round 2 had started. Why it cannot be solved now: the rule is that reproduction outputs are not edited after scoring. Approach: from the next reproduction on, the posts will have no marks, as the brief says.
- **The site ledger has no kind or search method.** `profiles/<author>/source-sites.raw.md` has only per-domain counts. Why it cannot be solved now: method re-extraction was stopped because the goal changed. Approach: have the R-layer work in the method-extraction worker brief fill in the kind, the internal search method, the language, and the paywall status.
- **The round-2 reproduction logs carry no `[검색]`/`[열람]` marker lines and their `## 요약` counts were tallied by the worker.** The marker format and `rek repro-stats` were introduced after those workers had started, so `rek repro-stats` reports 0 searches and pages for them. Cannot be fixed now: reproduction outputs are frozen once scored. From the next reproduction the brief enforces the markers.
