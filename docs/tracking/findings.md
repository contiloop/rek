# Unsolved problems

- **The rare-source verdict depends on the region of the search tool.** The workers' web search tool is US-based, so even Korean searches return almost no Korean outlets. As a result almost every domestic article is classified as rare (rare counts under the reference-author criteria: 32, 33, 39). Dozens of rare sources for one blog post means the definition is loose. Why it cannot be solved now: no tool available to workers returns Naver search results in the same format. Approach: fetch the top 10 domains of Naver News and integrated search by code, merge them into the set, and recount the rare sources.
- **Chrome extension tools were not visible in subagents.** Paid and 403 pages (로이드 리스트 (Lloyd's List), FT, Seatrade, and others) were reached only through summaries, reposts, and the Wayback Machine. Why it cannot be solved now: it appears to be a tool exposure problem in this session environment, and the conditions to reproduce it were not confirmed. Approach: rerun one source tracing in a session where the Chrome extension is visible, and check whether the `[inferred]` sources decrease.
- **The profile carries traces of the material posts and contaminates the tests.** The move-level re-extraction removed the material's nouns from every rule sentence, and the two new posts were tested before entering the material, so the old hold-out variants were deleted. What remains: shared documents still use material examples (the development-unit example in `evidence.md`, "청어 붕괴 → 북해 고등어 → …"), and `instances.md` lists the material's sites and queries as hints. Why it cannot be solved now: four material posts (three of them in resource, shipping and energy fields) are too few to tell author habit from field habit. Approach: trace posts from other fields and replace the examples in the shared documents with other topics.
- **Evidence marks remain in the body of the round 2 reproduced posts.** The brief was fixed after round 2 had started. Why it cannot be solved now: the rule is that reproduction outputs are not edited after scoring. Approach: from the next reproduction on, the posts will have no marks, as the brief says.
- **The site ledger has no kind or search method.** `profiles/<author>/source-sites.raw.md` has only per-domain counts. Why it cannot be solved now: method re-extraction was stopped because the goal changed. Approach: have the R-layer work in the method-extraction worker brief fill in the kind, the internal search method, the language, and the paywall status.
- **The round-2 reproduction logs carry no `[검색]`/`[열람]` marker lines and their `## 요약` counts were tallied by the worker.** The marker format and `rek repro-stats` were introduced after those workers had started, so `rek repro-stats` reports 0 searches and pages for them. Cannot be fixed now: reproduction outputs are frozen once scored. From the next reproduction the brief enforces the markers.
- **Image-caption facts were fabricated in the 고등어 reproductions and in the first two test posts.** The depth cycle added the `image: <description> ← S<nn>` line and the rule that the image's sources must state what it shows; after that one image description failed (224458617394 attempt 2) and none on the following two attempts. Why it stays listed: a sample of two attempts. Approach: watch the next runs; if it recurs, make the image line's quotation a required field.
- **Unsourced specifics inside sourced sentences survive three brief additions.** The pass line failed six times on fabrication counts of 4, 4, 1, 4, 1, 2; every item is a name, office, year, cause or epithet the worker adds from general knowledge to a fact that is itself sourced. Why it cannot be solved now: the brief already forbids it three ways; the worker's final check still misses one or two. Approach: a mandatory last step that tabulates every proper noun, office, year and causal clause of `post.md` with a source id (code could count the rows but not judge them), or a ruling from the user on name links the sources make unambiguous.
- **The author's posts are mirrored on an aggregator site (a third-party page per blog).** The tracer saw the mirror in search results. The test runs' `excluded_domains:` lines include it; the four material runs' headers do not (they were fixed before the mirror was found). Why it cannot be solved now: `units.md` headers are not edited after a reproduction. Approach: add the mirror to the header of any future run and to the tracer's instructions for the excluded list.
- **`units_total` of the same original varies by who splits it: comparison workers cut 15, 17, 18 units on one post and 20, 21, 22 on the other, and on 224314709934 the tracer cut 20 where a comparator had cut 24.** Each attempt's comparator splits the original anew without seeing earlier splits. Why it cannot be solved now: the brief forbids reading earlier attempts so that each comparison is independent, and the pass line held on every attempt by a wide margin anyway. Approach: when a post has a tracer's `units.md`, let the comparator use that table as the original side; for test-only runs accept the drift and compare depth ratios, not counts.
- **Two worker rule breaches in the depth cycle, both reported to the user at the time.** (1) The method-extraction worker ran one read-only `git status` while checking line counts (recorded in `profiles/reference-a/status.md`); nothing changed. (2) A reproduction worker (222863642965 attempt 1) put a fragment of the user's e-mail address into a User-Agent header on one request to Wikipedia. The researcher briefs now forbid personal data in request headers, and `security.md` records the rule. Why they stay listed: the briefs say "never run git" and the request rule is new; whether workers obey them is only known after more runs.
- **Brief edits landed while attempts were running.** The researcher brief was amended between attempts for each post's routing, and each post's next attempt ran with the amendments made for both posts (attempt 3 of 222863642965 started minutes before a further amendment for 224458617394 was committed). The brief version of each attempt is listed in status. Why it cannot be solved now: the attempts were sequential per post as the rule requires, but the brief is shared. Approach: freeze the brief for the duration of a post's attempt set, or record the brief's commit in `repro/<n>/`.
- **Coverage routing never triggers a fix.** Every comparison also routed uncovered original units, mostly to the reproduction (skipped rules) and once per post to method extraction (an A-layer rule judging the responsible public body against its own conduct). Only pass-line failures led to fixes. Why it cannot be solved now: the pass line is depth and fabrication by the user's decision; coverage is report-only. Approach: the user decides whether coverage routing should ever trigger a profile fix.
